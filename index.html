    How the debt-minimization algorithm works
    1. For each person compute net = total paid − total share owed (in integer paise/cents, so no floating-point drift).
    2. Positive net → creditor (is owed money). Negative net → debtor. Net values always sum to 0.
    3. Repeatedly take the biggest debtor and biggest creditor. The debtor pays min(|debt|, credit) to the creditor. At least one of them becomes fully settled each step.
    4. Stop when everyone is at 0. With n people this needs at most n − 1 payments, versus up to n(n − 1)/2 pairwise debts if each expense were repaid separately.

    Finding the true minimum is NP-hard (it reduces to partitioning into zero-sum subsets). The greedy approach runs in O(n log n) and is exact in most real-world cases.
